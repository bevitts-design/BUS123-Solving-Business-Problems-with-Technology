Harborside Medical Center

Should hospital leadership pay cash for a new MRI or finance part of the purchase?

Download the Excel starter · View the shared rubric

Business context

Harborside needs the same MRI under either option. Leadership wants to protect liquidity while limiting borrowing costs. Compare the immediate purchase and the following 12 months. Show total contractual interest separately over the full loan term.

Options. Pay cash and Partial loan.

Case data and assumptions

The 24 records are included in the workbook’s Inputs tab. The same records are linked to the CaseAnalysis table on the Analysis tab. Preserve the given option and row order.

Build the model and verify it

  • Opening cash plus borrowing less the MRI payment gives cash immediately after purchase. Roll cash forward monthly by adding operating cash flow and subtracting the loan payment.
  • The monthly operating cash figures already reflect routine cash receipts and payments. Exclude depreciation, taxes on the financing choice, loan fees, and other borrowing.
  • Calculate total loan interest as the sum of all contractual payments less principal. When testing the cash floor, include the immediate post-purchase balance and all 12 month-end balances.
  • Complete the outputs on Analysis and Summary. Show an independent result for partial loan monthly payment and a check of partial loan downside minimum cash. Record your method, comparison and conclusion on Summary.
