Same principal and positive annual rate: which basis produces more interest?

Ordinary interest. The smaller denominator makes the year fraction larger: 90/360 = 0.25, compared with 90/365 ≈ 0.2466.

The formula must match the stated model

Same $18,000 receipt after two years · same 6% annual rate

Simple interest

=18000/(1+6%*2)Denominator: 1 + rate × years$16,071.43

Annual compound interest

=18000/(1+6%)^2Denominator: (1 + rate)years$16,019.94

The challenge specifies annual compounding. Which denominator fits?

Use (1+rate)^years, or the equivalent PV() function. Simple discounting is valid for a simple-interest assumption; mixing the two models creates the mismatch.
