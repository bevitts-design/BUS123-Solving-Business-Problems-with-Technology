Common Mistake

A correct formula can still answer the wrong model

Mixed-model work

=future_payment/(1+rate*year)

Then labels the five-year stream “Excel DCF” and compares it with compound PV().

Problem: linear and compound assumptions are mixed.

→

Correction

=-PV(rate,year,0,future_payment)

Use the same compound-periodic model for every payment, then sum the positive magnitudes.

State: annual periods, 6% per annual period, all cash flows valued today.
