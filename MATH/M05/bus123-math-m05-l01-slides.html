Hourly pay

Overtime Changes the Slope

Regular hours stop at 40. Only hours above the threshold receive the premium rate.

=MIN(Hours,40)*Rate + MAX(Hours-40,0)*Rate*1.5Regular: 40 × $44 = $1,760
Overtime: 3 × $44 × 1.5 = $198Gross pay = $1,958

Straight-line method

One Annual Expense, Six Years of Service

Ultrasound cost: $84,000 · Residual value: $12,000 · Useful life: 6 years

=(Cost-ResidualValue)/UsefulLife($84,000 − $12,000) ÷ 6Annual depreciation = $12,000

Depreciation is not new cash leaving this year, and book value is not necessarily market value.

$84K cost$12K residual

Common mistake

One Year Is Not the Running Total

The mix-up

“Book value = cost − this year’s depreciation” works only in Year 1.

Book value = cost − accumulated depreciation

Year 3 relationship

Expense this year: $12,000
Accumulated after 3 years: $36,000
Book value: $84,000 − $36,000 = $48,000

Expense → one period
Accumulated → running total
Book value → remaining recorded cost
