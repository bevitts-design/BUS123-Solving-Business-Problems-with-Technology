Part 1 · Sales tax · your Math M06 hoodie store

Your hoodie store: separate sales from tax

From Math M06: 30 hoodies at $40 each. Now add a practice tax of 6% to the $40 price.

Tidal hoodie store
30 hoodies × $40$1,200
Sales tax at 6%$72
Cash collected$1,272

The store earned $1,200 in sales. The other $72 is tax collected for government.

Why divide by 1.06?
100% selling price + 6% tax = 106%. Divide the cash collected by 106% to recover sales.

Work backward from cash collected

Property Tax

Use the town’s rules · round the rate · calculate the amount owed

Property tax · key terms

How the town calculates property tax

Value used for tax

Market value: estimated selling price.
Assessment rate: percentage used for tax.
Assessed value: market value × that percentage.

Our practice town’s rule

Find the tax rate

Divide the town’s tax-funded budget by all properties’ assessed values added together. Round this rate to four decimal places before calculating tax.

Property decisions are operating decisions
Property tax adds to the cost of using this building
