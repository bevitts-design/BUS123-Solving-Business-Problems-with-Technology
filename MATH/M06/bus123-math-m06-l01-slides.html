Middle purchase

Which method assigns the most cost to sales? Which leaves the most inventory value? Explain using the rising purchase costs.

Rising costs explain the ranking

FIFO assigns older, lower costs to sales: lowest COGS and highest ending value. LIFO assigns newer, higher costs: highest COGS and lowest ending value. Weighted average lies between them.

The sale and total cost do not change. The method changes where the $1,000 is assigned. With the same revenue, lower COGS means more gross profit.
