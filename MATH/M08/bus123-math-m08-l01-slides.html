Reading reinforcement · reference

Three forces inform the rate; none chooses it for you

Opportunity costWhat could the cash earn elsewhere while we wait?
InflationHow might the same dollars' purchasing power change?
RiskHow certain are the amount and timing of payment?
Decision rule: use a rate that fits this cash flow, timing, and risk—not a rate assembled mechanically.
Rate selection is a judgment.
The documents and payer determine which risk matters.

Prompt: Which force would change most if the payer's credit quality weakened?

Common Mistake

Mixed-model work

=FV/(1+rate×time)

Then labels the five-year stream “Excel DCF” and compares it with compound PV().

Problem: linear and compound assumptions are mixed.

Correction

=-PV(rate,year,0,future_payment)

Use the same compound-periodic model for every payment, then sum the positive magnitudes.
