Common mistake

Check What the Number Actually Means

Expense ≠ running total

Subtracting one year’s expense gives book value only in Year 1.

Year 3: $84,000 − $36,000 = $48,000

Expense ≠ new cash payment

A lower annual depreciation estimate does not prove a cash saving or longer physical life.

Cost allocation is an estimate.
Replacement needs operating evidence.
