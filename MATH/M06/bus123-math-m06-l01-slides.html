Common mistake

Allocation does not rewrite financial reporting

“We allocated store rent to products, so rent becomes inventory cost.”

Internal view: allocate rent to understand department economics.

External view: classify costs under the applicable reporting rules.

Often inventoriable

Purchase price, freight-in, and other costs required to bring inventory to its present location and condition

Often period expense

Store rent, selling expenses, and general administrative costs
