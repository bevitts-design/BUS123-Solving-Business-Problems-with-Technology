Excise Tax

Read the scenario rule · preserve the base · test the formula

Excise tax · simplified scenario

A targeted tax with a supplied rule

What excise means here

One designated product

The training jurisdiction applies an excise tax to a specified commercial appliance. The problem supplies the rate.

Business impact

Higher acquisition cost

The tax changes the manager's cost and may affect price, margin, and demand.

Scenario rule

When the problem says both taxes share the original base, calculate both from that base. This is a lesson assumption, not a universal tax rule.

1 base

Two rates can point to the same starting amount without pointing to each other.

Compounding error

StepWhat you calculateExcel syntax firstManager check

Common mistake

Correct inputs can still produce the wrong liability

Wrong tax base

=MarketValue*RoundedRate

This skips the assessment step and overstates the taxable value.

=AssessedValue*RoundedRate

Use the output of step 1 as the base for step 3.

Wrong rounding point

=ROUND(RawRate*AssessedValue,2)

This carries an unrounded rate into the tax calculation.

=ROUND(Budget/TotalAV,4)

Round the rate first, then calculate annual tax.
