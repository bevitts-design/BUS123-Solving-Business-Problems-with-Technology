Massachusetts · why stores collect sales tax

The store collects tax for the state

Massachusetts law requires sellers to collect tax on taxable sales and send it to the Department of Revenue (DOR).

Customer

Pays tax
at checkout

→

State

Receives tax

Why use the business?

The seller already handles checkout. It collects tax with payment and reports it to the state. Collected tax is an amount owed, not sales revenue.

Where does the money go?

• The state’s general budget supports public services
• Dedicated revenue helps fund MBTA transit
• Dedicated revenue helps build and renovate public schools

Sources: State budget · MA DOR · Collection law · MBTA · School buildings

Check the item’s tax rule
MA’s general rate is 6.25%. Ordinary clothing up to $175 per item is generally exempt, so a $40 hoodie usually has no MA sales tax.
Part 1 · Sales tax · Tidal dorm essentials

Desk-lamp sales: separate sales from tax

Tidal sells 30 new desk lamps at $40 each in Massachusetts. Sales tax is 6.25%.

Tidal dorm essentials
30 desk lamps × $40$1,200
Sales tax at 6.25%$75
Cash collected$1,275

The store earned $1,200 in sales. The other $75 is tax collected for government.

Why divide by 1.0625?
100% selling price + 6.25% tax = 106.25%. Divide the cash collected by 106.25% to recover sales.
