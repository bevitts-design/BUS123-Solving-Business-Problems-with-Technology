BUS123 Capstone · Retail

Tidal Goods Co.

Which inventory order should the owner place for the upcoming eight-week selling period?

Download the Excel starter · View the shared rubric

Business context

Tidal Goods runs a coastal retail shop. The owner wants to capture seasonal sales without committing more cash than the store can spare. The two options cover the same 12 products, with different order quantities and the same unit costs.

Options. Lean order and Bulk order.

Case data and assumptions

The 24 records are included in the workbook’s Inputs tab. The same records are linked to the CaseAnalysis table on the Analysis tab. Preserve the given option and row order.

AssumptionValueHow to use it
Inventory cash budget$27,000Maximum cash available for the order.
Downside demand as a share of base80%Multiply expected demand by 80% and round down to whole units for each product.

Build the model and verify it

  • Assume no beginning inventory. Units sold equal the lower of order quantity and demand. Unsold units remain in inventory, with no salvage revenue or write-off during the eight-week period.
  • Cash committed is the cost of the full order. Cost of goods sold applies only to units sold. Gross profit is revenue less cost of goods sold. Calculate total gross margin from total gross profit divided by total revenue.
  • Compare cash committed with the budget and weigh the sales opportunity against cash tied up in unsold inventory. Use the supplied costs and prices throughout both scenarios.
  • Complete the outputs on Analysis and Summary. Show an independent result for lean order base gross profit and a check of bulk order budget headroom. Record your method, comparison and conclusion on Summary.

Summarize and visualize

  • PivotTable 1 • Rows: Group (product category). Columns: Option. Values: Sum of cash committed.
  • PivotTable 2 • Rows: Option. Values: Sum of base gross profit and sum of downside gross profit.
  • Chart the base and downside gross profit for both options. Build a second chart from PivotTable 1 that compares the options across product categories. Exclude grand totals from chart series.
  • Format currency, percentages, and counts appropriately. Use clear headers, readable column widths, consistent precision, meaningful titles, and labels or legends that identify the cases or series.
