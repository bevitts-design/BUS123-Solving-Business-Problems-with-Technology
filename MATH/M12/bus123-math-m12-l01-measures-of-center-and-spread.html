Arithmetic Mean · =AVERAGE(range)

Adds every observation and redistributes the total equally. Great for symmetric data, but sensitive to extreme outliers.

Median · =MEDIAN(range)

The middle value after sorting ascending. Resistant to outliers—preferred for skewed salaries, home prices, or wait times.

Mode · =MODE.SNGL(range)

The most frequent numeric value. In Clinic A, the mode is 20 minutes. Excel returns #N/A if no numbers repeat.

Standard Deviation · =STDEV.S(range)

Quantifies the typical distance of observations from the mean in the same units as your data (minutes, dollars, hours).

Mathematical Formulas: Mean, Variance, Standard Deviation & IQR Outliers
Sample Mean: x̄ = (Σ xᵢ) / n   |   Population Mean: μ = (Σ xᵢ) / N
Sample Variance: s² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n − 1)   →   Sample SD: s = √s²
Population Variance: σ² = Σ(xᵢ − μ)² / N   →   Population SD: σ = √σ²

Formal Outlier Check (IQR Rule): Find the Interquartile Range IQR = Q3 − Q1. Any observation below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR is flagged as a potential outlier requiring manager investigation (data-entry error vs. genuine extreme business event).

Why Divide Sample Variance by n − 1 Instead of n?

When the population mean μ is unknown, you measure deviations from the sample mean x̄. The sample mean minimizes the sum of squared deviations for those observations. Dividing that sum by n underestimates the population variance on average.

The deviations from the sample mean add up to zero. Once n − 1 deviations are known, the last one is fixed. Dividing by n − 1 gives an unbiased estimate of population variance for a random sample, which Excel calculates with =VAR.S(). Excel’s =STDEV.S() takes the square root to express spread in the original units. The resulting sample standard deviation can still be biased.

Excel Statistical Function Reference Card & Common Pitfalls
Function Excel Syntax Business Use & Important Check
Mean =AVERAGE(C8:G8) Arithmetic mean; watch for distortion from extreme outliers.
Median =MEDIAN(C8:G8) Middle rank value; ideal for skewed income, real estate, or wait times.
Mode =MODE.SNGL(B2:B8) Numeric data only; returns #N/A if no number repeats. For text categories, use PivotTables or =COUNTIF().
Multiple Modes =MODE.MULT(B2:B8) Spills all tied numeric modes in Microsoft 365 when datasets are bimodal.
Sample SD =STDEV.S(range) Default for business forecasting when inferring a broader ongoing process.
Population SD =STDEV.P(range) Use only when data captures every single unit in the defined target group.
Range Bounds =MIN(range) / =MAX(range) Reasonableness check: the mean and median must always fall between MIN and MAX.
